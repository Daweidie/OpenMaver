HudFontAtlas*.png contain rasterized glyphs from:

- Lucida Sans Typewriter (Lucida is a registered trademark or trademark of
  Bigelow & Holmes in the U.S. and other countries), and
- Noto Sans CJK SC (Copyright Adobe and Google).

Noto Sans CJK is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full license
text is in OFL.txt. Only the generated atlas bitmaps are packaged into the mod
jar, not the original font files. The Lucida TTF used to regenerate the atlases
lives in tools/fonts/ and is intentionally kept out of mc/resources.
